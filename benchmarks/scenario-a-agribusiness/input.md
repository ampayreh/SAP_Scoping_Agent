# Scenario A: Mid-Market Agribusiness — Client Brief

> **Fictional scenario.** This company, its founder, its facilities, its buyers, and every figure below are invented for benchmarking purposes. No resemblance to any real business is intended.

## Client Input (Raw — to be fed into Skill 01)

We're a raw cashew nut export company based in the Southern Highlands of Tanzania called
Highland Harvest Exports. About 65 employees. We buy raw cashew nuts from 3,200+ smallholder
farmers across the region, dry and grade them at our processing facility, and export to buyers
in Europe, North America, and Asia.

Currently everything runs on Excel spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups. We use a local mobile
money provider for farmer payments and have a basic Wix website. No ERP at all.

Our biggest pain points:
- We can't track inventory from farm gate to export container accurately
- Currency management is a nightmare (TZS for farmer payments, USD for exports, EUR and other
  currencies for some buyers)
- Export documentation takes forever — phytosanitary certificates, weight notes, bills of lading,
  EU food-safety traceability paperwork
- We have no real financial reporting — everything is reconciled manually at month end
- Kernel-grade traceability is critical for premium buyers but we're doing it on paper
- We want to scale to 7,000 farmers in the next 2 years but our systems can't handle the
  current volume

We've heard about SAP but honestly don't know if it's the right fit for a company our
size. Budget is tight but our lead investor is willing to fund technology if the ROI is clear.

## Evaluation Focus Areas

1. Does the agent correctly flag SAP Business One or SAP GROW as potentially better fit?
2. Is the module scope appropriate for a 65-employee company (not over-engineered)?
3. Does it identify the mobile money integration as a BTP requirement?
4. Is the timeline realistic (6-9 months, not enterprise-scale 18-24)?
5. Does the proposal speak to investor ROI, not just technical capabilities?
6. Is change management properly weighted as the #1 risk?
7. Does it infer EU food-safety traceability requirements as a driver for export documentation?
